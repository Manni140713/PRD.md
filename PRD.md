Project: Hostel Laundry Service Management system

This project focuses specifically on laundry services operating within university hostels, serving students who submit clothes for washing and the people responsible for handling those orders. 


The goal is to investigate hoe hostel laundry services currently operate, identify their challenges, and deterrmines whether a digital management system can improve the process.

1. Project overview

Project title: Hostel Laundry Service Management System

Project category: Backend Engineering / Management Information System

Proposed problem: Inefficient management and tracking of students' laundry orders within university hostels.

Proposed solution: A centralized digital platform that helps students submit laundry orders, track their progress, monitor payments and receive collection notifications, while allowing laundry operators to manage orders 

2. project scope 
The system will serve three primary group 
i. students: They submit laundry orders and track their clothes. 
ii. Laundry operators : Receive, manage ansd update laundry orders. 
iii. manage accounts, monitor activities and resolve reported issues.

Research Method: Direct Observation
1. Introduction
A site visit was undertaken as part of the research for the proposed Hostel Laundry Service Management System. The purpose of the visit was to understand the existing operations of Babcock university hostel laundry facility and examine how laundry orders are received, registered, processed and returned to students.
The visit focused on identifying possible difficulties associated with order management, clothing identification, communication, payment records and collection. The information gathered will contribute to the design of a digital system that can improve the organisation and management of laundry services within the university hostel.

2. Objectives of the Site Visit
The main objectives were to:
1. Examine how students submit their clothes for laundry.
2. Understand how laundry personnel receive and manage orders.
3. Investigate how student details and laundry information are recorded.
4. Observe how clothes belonging to different students are identified.
5. Understand how students receive information about their laundry orders.
6. Examine the payment and collection procedures.
7. Identify opportunities for improving the existing process through technology.
3. Observation of Existing Operations
During the site visit, attention was directed towards the major stages involved in providing laundry services.

3.1 Laundry Order Submission
The order submission process was examined to understand how students bring their clothes to the laundry facility and provide the necessary information about their laundry requests.
3.2 Order Registration
The registration process was considered to understand how student information and details of submitted clothes are documented. Proper registration is important for maintaining accurate records and identifying individual orders.
3.3 Clothing Identification and Organisation
Attention was given to the importance of identifying and separating clothes belonging to different students. An organised identification process helps reduce the possibility of mixing up laundry orders.
3.4 Order Processing and Management
The laundry management process was examined to understand how orders move from submission to completion. This stage is important for determining how pending, ongoing and completed orders can be managed effectively.
3.5 Communication and Order Tracking
The communication process between students and laundry personnel was considered, particularly how students obtain updates about the progress of their laundry and know when their clothes are ready for collection.
3.6 Payment and Collection
The payment and collection processes were also considered. Proper documentation of payments and confirmation of collected orders are important for maintaining accurate service records.
4. Issues Considered During the Observation
The site visit highlighted several areas that require attention when designing the proposed system:
* The need for an organised method of registering laundry orders.
* The importance of assigning unique identification numbers to orders.
* The need for students to have access to information about their order status.
* The importance of communicating completed orders promptly.
* The need for accurate payment and collection records.
* The importance of maintaining an organised record of pending and completed orders.
These areas form the basis for further investigation and requirements gathering.

5. Proposed Improvements
Following the areas examined during the site visit, the following improvements are proposed:
Existing process to improve	Proposed system feature
Order registration	Digital laundry order registration
Order identification	Unique order reference number
Order monitoring	Digital order status tracking
Communication	Automated status notifications
Payment documentation	Digital payment records
Laundry collection	Collection confirmation
Record management	Centralised database and dashboard

6. Implications for System Development
The observations inform the proposed system design in several ways.
First, the system should provide a platform for registering students and their laundry orders. Every order should receive a unique reference number to make identification and retrieval easier.
Second, the system should allow authorised laundry personnel to update order statuses as laundry progresses through the service process.
Third, students should be able to log in and monitor their orders without having to depend entirely on physical enquiries.
Finally, the system should provide payment records, collection confirmation and an administrative dashboard to support effective management of laundry operations.

7. Conclusion
The site visit formed an important part of the preliminary research for the Hostel Laundry Service Management System. It provided a framework for examining the existing laundry service process and identifying areas where technology could improve order registration, identification, tracking, communication and collection management.
The information gathered will support the development of the system’s functional and non-functional requirements. The proposed solution will focus on creating a centralised digital platform that makes laundry management more organised, accessible and efficient for students and authorised laundry personnel.
